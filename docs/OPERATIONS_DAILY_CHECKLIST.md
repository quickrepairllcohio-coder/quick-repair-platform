# Daily Operations Checklist

## Morning
- Review emergency queue
- Review technician availability and compliance expirations
- Review today's schedule and conflicts
- Review unpaid/failed payments
- Review inventory below reorder point

## During day
- Monitor emergency dispatches
- Monitor late arrivals
- Monitor failed SMS/push notifications
- Resolve offline-sync conflicts
- Approve exceptions/change orders

## End of day
- Reconcile Stripe payments against invoices
- Review completed jobs and missing completion reports
- Review refunds/tips
- Review technician hours and material usage
- Review ledger balance and unresolved errors
- Export operational/financial backup report
